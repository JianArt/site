I'm Jian Adornado.

From data to decisions—empowering clear, actionable insights for everyone.

I am a highly experienced product designer specializing in data visualization, monitoring, and operational dashboards. With over 15 years of experience, I’ve had the opportunity to work on a wide range of high-profile projects at some of the leading tech companies. My expertise lies in creating intuitive and impactful designs that make complex data more accessible and actionable for users, enabling informed decision-making across industries.

At Netflix, I led the design of data products aimed at explaining and monitoring key performance metrics for titles and casts, delivering actionable insights that informed strategic decisions. At Uber, I developed visualization tools for operational and financial data, and designed mission-critical systems to manage and optimize autonomous vehicle operations. Additionally, I played a key role in designing the next-generation Amazon Appstore for Android, which significantly enhanced user engagement and influenced the development of other Amazon product lines.

As a Product Design Manager at Dropbox, I contributed to the design of core products that drove substantial revenue growth. My experience also includes work in the fintech space, where I served as the founding designer for a stealth fintech startup and developed trading platform applications for OANDA.

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, I lead innovative design initiatives and currently serve as a Principal Product Designer, Data Visualization at Ōura, where I help design complex health data into clear, empowering insights for everyday life.
